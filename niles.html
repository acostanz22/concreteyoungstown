Concrete Contractors in Niles, Ohio

Niles sits between Youngstown and Warren. The driveways are a mix of older concrete and asphalt on city lots, with the same salt and frost heave as the rest of the metro. Most replacement jobs start with one question: does the slab come out, or is any of it sound enough to keep?

What changes on a Niles lot

We check the garage floor, the sidewalk, and the drainage before we answer that. A new drive that is too high at the door, or poured over a slab that is still moving, will crack in the old pattern. Stain is only a topic if the patio you want colored is still solid.

Questions

Niles concrete questions

Can you match a new Niles driveway to the garage floor?

Yes. Meeting the garage without a lip, and still draining away from it, is part of the layout. If an old overlay built the drive up too high, we remove that and start from a height that works.

Do you remove the failed slab, or pour over it?

We remove it when it is cracked through, rocking, or holding water. Pouring over that kind of slab is a short-lived cover. A panel that is solid and at the right elevation can sometimes stay and be tied to the new concrete.

Is stained concrete worth it on a Niles patio that already has cracks?

Only if the cracks are tight joints and the slab is stable. Stain will not glue a separated patio back together, and it will darken every patch. If you are unsure, ask us to look before you pick a color.

How soon can we park on a new driveway?

Several days for a car, longer when nights are cold. We set the day from the pour and the forecast before we leave Niles. Walking on it happens sooner than parking on it.
