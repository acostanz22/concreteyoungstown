Concrete Service Areas around Youngstown

Concrete Youngstown works in Youngstown and the towns around it in Mahoning County and nearby Trumbull County. Each page below is about the flatwork on those lots: access, drainage, salt, and the kind of slab we are usually replacing. It is not a town history.

Call (330) 293-2914
Boardman, Ohio

Boardman sits directly south of Youngstown, with longer residential drives and commercial frontage along Route 224.

Austintown, Ohio

Austintown is west of Youngstown, a mix of ranch-house streets and commercial lots along Mahoning Avenue. Driveways here take salt from a busy township, and many of the slabs are original to mid-century houses.

Canfield, Ohio

Canfield is southwest of Youngstown, with village streets and larger township lots. Driveways run longer, and more of the calls are patios and decorative walks rather than a short city apron.

Poland, Ohio

Poland is southeast of Youngstown. Village streets can be tight for a concrete truck, and township properties outside the village often have more grade and a longer approach.

Struthers, Ohio

Struthers is east of Youngstown, with older houses and shorter driveways. Many pours are garage aprons, narrow drives, and private walks where a truck cannot pull in.

Warren, Ohio

Warren is north of Youngstown in Trumbull County, close enough that we treat it as part of the regular service area.

Niles, Ohio

Niles sits between Youngstown and Warren. The driveways are a mix of older concrete and asphalt on city lots, with the same salt and frost heave as the rest of the metro.

Girard, Ohio

Girard is a small city just north of Youngstown, between the city and Niles. Lots are often modest, drives are short, and the edges of older slabs crumble where salt and plows hit them.

Hubbard, Ohio

Hubbard is east of Youngstown, toward the Pennsylvania line. Residential drives and patios here sit on the clay soils typical of this side of the valley.

Campbell, Ohio

Campbell is on Youngstown’s southeast side, with older houses on smaller lots. Driveways are narrow, staging room is limited, and a lot of the concrete has reached the end of a hard life with salt and frost.

Youngstown itself

In the city, drives are often shorter, trucks have less room, and the concrete has already lived through a lot of salt. The service pages for driveways, patios, stamped concrete, stained concrete, asphalt, and other flatwork are written for that work. If your street is in Youngstown, start there or send the form.
