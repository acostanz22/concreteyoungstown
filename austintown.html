Concrete Contractors in Austintown, Ohio

Austintown is west of Youngstown, a mix of ranch-house streets and commercial lots along Mahoning Avenue. Driveways here take salt from a busy township, and many of the slabs are original to mid-century houses. The failure we see most is a cracked apron at the street and a drive that has settled out of plane with the garage.

Questions

Austintown concrete questions

Can you replace a cracked apron on a busy Austintown street?

Yes, when the apron is private work and we can form it to the sidewalk and the gutter. We keep a path for cars that still need the street, and we will tell you if the public walk next to the apron is a city repair rather than part of our pour.

Do you pour small parking pads along Mahoning Avenue?

Yes. A pad for a shop or a house still needs thickness for the vehicles that will use it, and it has to drain without sending water at the building or across the sidewalk in a sheet. Tell us what parks there.

What if the old drive sits higher than the sidewalk?

We cut the replacement down to a slope that meets the walk without a bump or a pond. Building the new drive on top of a high slab usually makes the garage and the sidewalk worse. Removal is the normal path.

Can you work if a car has to stay until pour day?

Yes. Tell us that when we measure. We plan the breakout and the pour so you are not blocked longer than the work requires. Once the new concrete is down, the car stays off it until it can take the weight.
