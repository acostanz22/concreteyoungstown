Maintaining Stamped Concrete in Ohio

Cleaning and periodic sealing may help preserve appearance. Snow, ice, deicers, and freeze-thaw cycles can affect decorative surfaces. Select a suitable texture and follow the contractor's care recommendations. For color without a stamped texture, see stained concrete.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is stamped concrete more expensive than regular concrete?

Generally yes, because stamping involves extra labor, textures, coloring, and materials. The estimate reflects the pattern and finish you choose.

How long does stamped concrete last?

It can last decades with proper installation and care; climate, drainage, and traffic affect lifespan. Follow the care recommendations for your finish.

Can existing concrete be stamped?

Traditional stamping is done on fresh concrete. Some existing slabs may be suitable for a specialized decorative overlay after evaluation.

Does stamped concrete become slippery?

Certain smooth or sealed finishes can be slippery when wet or icy. Surface texture matters.

Does stamped concrete need to be sealed?

Sealing is often recommended, with timing based on product, exposure, and use. Ask about a maintenance plan when the surface is installed.
