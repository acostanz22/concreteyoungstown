About Concrete Youngstown

We are a service-area concrete crew for Youngstown and the towns around it. Driveways, patios, stamped and stained flatwork, sidewalks, small pads, and asphalt paving. There is no showroom and no public storefront. The estimate starts with the slab you have.

What we do on a job

We look at the concrete, the grade, and the access. If the slab has failed, we say replacement. If a panel can stay, we say that too. Decorative work is offered when the concrete is new or still sound, not as a cover for damage.

Winters here are part of the plan. Freeze-thaw, road salt, and clay soils that hold water show up on driveways from Youngstown to Warren. Joints, base, and slope matter more than a slogan on a truck.

We do not publish a street address, a license number, a warranty term, or a year founded, because those are not things to invent for a webpage. If you need a document for a permit or a property manager, ask when we write the estimate and we will tell you what we can provide.
