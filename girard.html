Concrete Contractors in Girard, Ohio

Girard is a small city just north of Youngstown, between the city and Niles. Lots are often modest, drives are short, and the edges of older slabs crumble where salt and plows hit them. We replace drives, pour walks to detached garages, and put in pads where a side yard is the only way to move material.

What changes on a Girard lot

Crumbling edges are usually a base and a joint problem, sometimes with too little thickness at the unsupported side. Filling the edge with a skim will not hold. We cut back to sound concrete or replace the panel, then form an edge that has stone under it.

Questions

Girard concrete questions

Can you get forms and concrete into a tight Girard side yard?

If a wheelbarrow fits, we can usually buggy concrete through. If the opening is narrower than that, or a fence has to come down, we will say so before you schedule a pour. Access changes the price more than the finish does.

Do you replace only the broken panels of a driveway?

Yes, when the other panels are solid, level enough, and worth keeping. We cut at the joints and dowel into the sound concrete. If every bay is cracked, replacing one leaves you with a new square in a failed drive.

What causes the edges of a Girard driveway to crumble?

Salt, freeze-thaw, and an edge with little base under it. Plows and tires finish the job. The repair is a new edge or a new panel with stone underneath, not a surface coat on loose concrete.

Can you pour a walk from the back door to a detached garage?

Yes. We slope it so it is not a river between the two buildings, and we joint it where it meets the existing slabs. Tell us if you need it wide enough for a mower, not only for a person.
