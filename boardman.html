Concrete Contractors in Boardman, Ohio

Boardman sits directly south of Youngstown, with longer residential drives and commercial frontage along Route 224. The concrete work is the same valley climate: freeze-thaw, road salt, and soils that hold water. What changes is the lot. Subdivision drives are often longer than a city lot in Youngstown, and a patio behind the house has to share the yard with drainage from a larger roof.

What changes on a Boardman lot

We replace scaled driveways, pour patios that fall away from the foundation, and patch or replace aprons that have broken at the street. A Boardman drive that slopes back toward the garage is a layout problem, not a sealing problem. We measure that fall before we talk about broom finish or stamp.

Questions

Boardman concrete questions

Can you replace a long Boardman driveway that slopes toward the garage?

Yes. The new grade has to leave the garage and still meet the sidewalk or the street. On a long drive that sometimes means correcting a belly in the middle, not just raising the garage end. We will show you the fall before we form it.

Do you pour patios behind houses in the Boardman subdivisions?

Yes. We lay the patio out against the door, the yard drainage, and whatever you need to keep, including basement windows. A larger suburban yard does not change the rule that water has to move away from the house.

How do you get concrete into a Boardman backyard with a narrow side gate?

If the truck can reach the street side, we buggy the concrete through the gate when the opening is wide enough. If it is not, we say so and price the access we actually have. A gate that looks wide enough for a mower is often tight for a loaded buggy.

Is asphalt or concrete more practical on a Boardman driveway?

Both are on the table. Concrete is the usual replacement when the old slab has scaled and you want a rigid drive. Asphalt fits some lots where a flexible surface and a faster return to parking matter more. We will tell you which one the base and the slope support.
