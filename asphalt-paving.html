Asphalt Paving

Asphalt Paving in Youngstown, Ohio

Asphalt is a flexible pavement. It can be a practical driveway or small parking area when the lot needs a surface that can flex a little and still shed water. Standard asphalt is not a porous drain. If the grade sends water at the garage, a new black surface will send it there too. We fix the slope as part of the paving, not as an afterthought.

When asphalt paving fits

  • An old asphalt drive has alligator cracking, potholes, or edges that have crumbled back to the stone.
  • A gravel drive ruts every spring and you want a paved surface for two cars, not a structural concrete slab.
  • A small parking pad beside a shop or a house needs paving, and concrete is not the better fit for how the base moves.

Water has to leave the surface

Dense asphalt sheds water. It does not soak it up. The base under it still has to be stable, and the surface still has to slope away from buildings. A pretty overlay on a sunken, wet base will crack in the same pattern as the pavement it covered.

How the work goes

01

Look at the failure

Alligator cracks usually mean the base, not just the top. A few potholes in an otherwise sound drive are a different repair than a full mill and pave. We say which one you are looking at.

02

Grade and base

Soft material comes out. Stone goes down and is compacted. Edges are held so the mat does not ravel at the grass.

03

Pave and compact

Asphalt is placed and rolled while it is hot enough to compact. Thickness follows the use: a car drive is not a dumpster lane.

04

Stay off it while it cools

You can usually walk a new mat sooner than you can park on it. Hot weather keeps it tender longer. We will tell you when to pull the car back in.

What changes the estimate

We do not publish a price per square foot. These are the items that move a quote once we see the lot.

Area and thickness

Square footage and how many inches of asphalt the traffic needs are the core of the price. A thin skin over a failed base is not a bargain.

Removal

Milling or tearing out old asphalt, and hauling it, is separate from the new tonnage.

Base stone

A gravel drive that was never built as a pavement often needs stone and grading before the first pass of asphalt.

Edges and access

Paving against a garage, a fence, or a tight turn from the street takes more hand work than an open rectangle.

Drainage

Rebuilding a swale, raising a settled center, or keeping water out of a basement stair is part of the job when the lot requires it.

Before you schedule

Should I choose asphalt or concrete for a Youngstown driveway?

Concrete is rigid. It wants a stable base and joints, and it handles summer heat without going soft. Asphalt flexes more, which can suit a base that moves a little, and it is usually faster to put back in service. Both fail if water sits under them. If your lot could go either way, we will talk through it on the visit rather than picking from a slogan.

Can you pave over the old asphalt?

Only if the old mat is stable, the edges are sound, and adding height will not block the garage door or send water at the house. Alligator cracking and a soft base need to come out. An overlay on that kind of failure copies the cracks into the new surface.

How soon can we drive on new asphalt?

Often the same day or the next, once the mat has cooled and set, but a hot afternoon can keep it tender. Turning the steering wheel in place will scuff a fresh drive. We will give you a time before we leave, matched to the weather that day.

Do you fill potholes, or only repave the whole drive?

A tight pothole in a sound drive can be patched. A drive that is a map of cracks needs replacement, because the patch will sit in the same failed base. We would rather tell you that on the visit than sell a patch that will open up by spring.

Does asphalt drain water through the surface?

Ordinary driveway asphalt does not. It is paved to shed water to the side or the street. If someone told you asphalt is a porous drainage system, that is a different product than a standard residential mat. Your drive still needs slope.

When would you seal an asphalt drive?

Sealer is maintenance on a sound mat, usually after the asphalt has cured, not on the day it is paved. It is not a fix for cracks that go through the mat or for a base that has failed. If you need sealing later, ask when we look at the drive and we will tell you whether the surface is a candidate.
