Residential & Commercial Asphalt Paving

Asphalt Driveway Installation

A properly graded and compacted asphalt driveway creates a functional, finished entrance to your home. Base preparation and drainage matter as much as the surface layer. Comparing surfaces? See concrete driveway installation.

Asphalt Driveway Replacement

Severe cracking, potholes, uneven sections, and base failure may make replacement preferable to repeated patching. Evaluate underlying conditions before resurfacing.

Small Commercial Asphalt Paving

Asphalt is suitable for certain parking areas and access lanes. Thickness and base design should reflect vehicle loads and expected traffic.

Benefits of Asphalt Paving

  • Often lower upfront installation cost than concrete.
  • Smooth, uniform appearance.
  • Flexible pavement structure.
  • Localized repairs may be possible.
  • May be opened to traffic sooner than new concrete when conditions allow.

Asphalt Paving for Northeast Ohio Weather

Water entering cracks can worsen asphalt damage during freezing weather. Proper grading and drainage reduce standing water, while timely repairs help limit deterioration. Asphalt also needs ongoing care appropriate for seasonal conditions.

Asphalt Repair or Replacement?

Isolated potholes and minor cracks may be repairable. Extensive alligator cracking, settling, or drainage issues can indicate base failure and may require reconstruction instead of an overlay.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does asphalt paving cost?

Price depends on area, thickness, base work, removal, access, and drainage. We price after reviewing the existing pavement and grade.

How long does an asphalt driveway last?

Many residential asphalt driveways last around 15–25 years, depending on climate, use, construction, and upkeep. Timely repairs help limit further damage.

Is asphalt cheaper than concrete?

Asphalt generally costs less up front, but maintenance and lifespan should also be considered. Choose based on budget, use, and long-term care.

How soon can I drive on new asphalt?

It depends on temperatures, installation details, and paving conditions. Follow your installer’s instructions.

Can asphalt be installed over an existing driveway?

Sometimes, if the existing pavement and base are structurally sound. Severe damage may call for removal and replacement.
