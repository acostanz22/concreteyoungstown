Custom Concrete Patio Designs

Concrete patios can be customized in size, shape, layout, and appearance.

Traditional Concrete Patios

A broom-finished concrete patio provides a clean, functional surface for furniture, grills, and backyard activities. The texture can improve traction and requires relatively little maintenance.

Stamped Concrete Patios

Stamped concrete creates decorative patterns resembling stone, slate, brick, and flagstone, giving outdoor spaces a distinctive appearance. Explore stamped concrete installation.

Decorative Concrete Patios

Borders, colors, and textured finishes can help coordinate a patio with surrounding paths, landscaping, and your home. Concrete staining is another decorative option for suitable surfaces.

Benefits of Installing a Concrete Patio

  • Creates a dedicated area for outdoor dining and entertaining.
  • Provides a stable surface for furniture and grills.
  • Offers different shapes, patterns, and finishes.
  • Requires relatively little routine maintenance.
  • Complements landscaping and other outdoor improvements.

Planning the Right Patio for Your Backyard

Think about how you'll use the space, furniture placement, access from the house, sunlight, landscaping, and drainage. Proper grading should direct water away from your home. Larger entertainment areas may need more room and a different layout than a small seating patio.

Concrete Patio Replacement

Older patios sometimes crack, settle, or develop drainage issues. Small areas may be repairable, while widespread damage can make replacement the better choice. Replacement also offers an opportunity to resize the patio and improve the layout and finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a concrete patio cost?

Price depends on square footage, site preparation, access, thickness, and decorative finish. Request an estimate based on your backyard.

Is stamped concrete good for patios?

Yes. It's a popular decorative option for patios, with patterns that resemble natural materials.

How long does a concrete patio last?

A properly installed patio can last decades, depending on drainage, climate, use, and care. Maintenance and site conditions affect that timeline.

Can a patio be installed on uneven ground?

Yes, but excavation, grading, and base preparation may be required. Those steps are part of planning a stable patio surface.

Does a concrete patio need maintenance?

Regular cleaning and drainage maintenance help. Decorative finishes may need periodic resealing.
